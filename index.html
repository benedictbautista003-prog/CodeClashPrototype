HOW TO PLAY

  1. 01 Play local on one screen, or go online and battle a friend on another device.
  2. 02 Every round shows one programming question to both players at the same time.
  3. 03 Race the clock. The first player to lock in the correct answer wins the round and scores a point.
  4. 04 A wrong answer doesn't end your round — the other player can still win it while your side shows "locked out."
  5. 05 If the timer hits zero with no correct answer, the round is a draw. No points awarded.
  6. 06 For online play: one player creates a lobby and shares the code, the other joins with it. The host runs the match and advances rounds.
  7. 07 After a fixed number of rounds, the higher score wins the match.
